# Privacy Policy for TODO BIRD

**Effective Date:** October 21, 2026  
**Developer / Publisher:** Duvorixen  
**Application:** TODO BIRD Desktop Application  

---

### 1. Overview
TODO BIRD is an offline-first desktop personal assistant, project planner, and task manager. We believe your personal schedules, tasks, and notes belong entirely to you. This Privacy Policy describes how TODO BIRD handles your data.

### 2. Information Collection and Storage
- **Local Storage Only:** All tasks, projects, WBS hierarchies, notes, and calendar data created or managed in TODO BIRD are stored exclusively on your local computer in a local SQLite database.
- **No External Servers:** TODO BIRD does not operate external tracking servers, telemetry services, or cloud synchronization. Your data never leaves your device.
- **Offline Voice Processing:** All voice companion and text-to-speech features operate entirely on your local machine using bundled voice engines. No voice data or audio recordings are transmitted over the internet.

### 3. Analytics and Telemetry
- TODO BIRD does **not** collect, track, log, or sell any personal data, device identifiers, or usage analytics.

### 4. Permissions Used
- **File System:** TODO BIRD requires read/write access to its own local application data folder to save your tasks, settings, and database backups.
- **Audio Output:** Used strictly for playing reminder chimes and local voice assistant responses through your local speakers/headphones.

### 5. Third-Party Services
TODO BIRD does not integrate with any third-party advertising networks, data brokers, or external analytics providers.

### 6. Data Retention and Deletion
Since all data is stored locally on your device, you maintain complete control over your data at all times. You can modify, export, or delete your tasks and database directly within the application or by uninstalling the application and removing its data folder.
