1. **Interactive WBS & Critical Path Method (CPM) Gantt Engine**
   * Multi-level parent/subtask hierarchies with automatic rollup calculation, slack analysis, and Critical Path highlighting.
   * **1-Click Sample Executive Project**: Load a complete multi-phase product launch schedule instantly to explore WBS & Gantt workflows.
2. **Guided 7-Step Spotlight Onboarding & Searchable `F1` User Manual**
   * First-time users get an interactive spotlight tour highlighting every core view, plus an 8-chapter searchable in-app manual (`F1`).
3. **Avocet Offline AI Voice Companion & Focus Studio**
   * Time-of-day spoken greetings, task briefings, integrated Pomodoro Focus Timer, and Multi-Timezone World Clock.
4. **100% Local-First Privacy**
   * All tasks, schedules, and analytics reside strictly in your local SQLite database. No cloud account required.
5. **10 Native Languages**
   * Full localization across English, Spanish, French, German, Hindi, Japanese, Chinese, Arabic (RTL), Russian, and Portuguese.
